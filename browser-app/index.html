BOUNDARYLAB · WORKSPACE GUIDE

Your image workspace

Explore image boundaries in a private, browser-based studio. Add an image, adjust the processing controls, then export the result.

01

Load an image

Open an image, drop it into the page, try the sample, or capture one with your camera.

02

Compare three frames

Color shows your original, Grayscale shows intensity, and Processed shows the selected output style.

03

Tune the edges

Choose Sobel, Canny-like, or Laplacian detection. Adjust contrast, brightness, smoothing, thickness, rotation, and edge color.

04

Save your work

Keep images in your private on-device gallery, then export a PNG, JPEG, WebP, or SVG edge map. Undo and Redo revisit recent changes.
