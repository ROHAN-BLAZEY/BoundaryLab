WHAT IS BOUNDARYLAB?

Accessible image processing, built around the image itself.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you begin.

Which image formats can I use?

The Studio accepts images supported by your browser, including JPG, PNG, WebP, and the first frame of GIF files.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The browser edition processes the selected image locally. Its optional gallery stays in browser storage on the current device.

Which exports are available?

PNG, JPEG, WebP, and an SVG edge export are available from the Studio. Export behavior depends on the selected output.

Do I need an account?

No. Core browser processing does not use sign-in or accounts.
